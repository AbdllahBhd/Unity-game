What differs from Unity?

Positions in the Current Unity collection come from your Unity level descriptions. This browser uses its own collision and pursuit model, not Unity Physics2D. Boss patterns, cannons and moving objects are approximations. Browser success does not verify Unity or phone performance.

The redesign rewards escape, no deaths and launch par independently. All pumpkins still seal the door.

Read the room

Glowing pumpkins hunt you. A fast impact or a dash smashes them; a slow touch is fatal. Land on broad platform tops to launch again. A death costs a leap.

Trajectory dots show free flight only. Moving obstacles can change the outcome.
